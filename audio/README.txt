Put your Alert voice file here:

  G:\V380-decoder\v380-snapshot-app\audio\alert.wav

Best format:
  - WAV
  - 8000 Hz
  - 16-bit
  - mono

A 44100 Hz stereo WAV also works; the app converts it.

Name must be exactly:  alert.wav

Then in Live View: Connect → Alert ON
The camera speaker will play this file.
